# Changelog

What changed in each version of `painel-sessao`. People who installed the plugin receive a new version only when its number changes.

## Unreleased

## 0.2.0 (2026-10-09)

First public version.

- The panel in Portuguese (Brazil) or English: option `language`.
- Times in the zone of the option `timeZone`, or of the system when it is empty.
- Options `openOnStart` and `answerLine` turn off the pane opening at session start and the line under each answer.
- Auto-send: a prompt that does not enter is sent again; it waits for the app's survey; it turns itself off after 3 failures and says why.
- Queued prompts and lines of the notes can be moved up and down.
- Copy buttons for messages and for the whole panel.
- A call of Claude that turns auto-send on asks for permission.
